Three things that surprise people.
The beam is not along z at x=y=0 — it is tilted ~6.4°, within ~12 mm of the axis near the entrance but ~95 mm off by the pad plane.

The vertex is not at (0,0) — vertices sit a median 50 mm off axis, because the beam is itself offset and has a finite spot.

The arms are not back-to-back. Equal-mass elastic gives a 90° opening in the lab; 180° is the centre-of-mass picture.

Run 128, branch FairRootv18.00-fairroot19-port. Events are clustered with direction-aware HDBSCAN and kept only if the opening angle falls in the 80–100° elastic band: scattering off the C and O in the CO₂ populates 60–70° and would confuse the topology. Vertices are fitted from all three segments. De-tilting uses the beam axis measured at B = 0 in run 100 (5.44°, −173.2°), not the parameter file's TiltAng/ThetaRot. The grey cylinder is the 1 m active volume: the beam enters at z≈1000 mm and drifts toward the pad plane at z≈0.
